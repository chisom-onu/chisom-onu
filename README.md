# Chisom Onu

### Customer Success Manager | Onboarding, Retention & Account Management

**I catch what's going quiet before it turns into a churn conversation.**

I help businesses turn customer signals into action across onboarding, adoption, account health, retention, and growth.

## The Aviation Edge

Before applying risk thinking to customer accounts, I applied it to aircraft operations.

As a licensed Flight Dispatcher and aviation operations professional, I learned to monitor multiple moving parts, recognize anomalies early, coordinate stakeholders under pressure, document accurately, and escalate before small issues become operational problems.

Customer Success relies on a surprisingly similar instinct.

A customer rarely becomes at risk overnight.

Usage changes.  
Engagement drops.  
Onboarding stalls.  
Stakeholders go quiet.  
Value becomes unclear.

**I pay attention before those signals turn into a churn conversation.**
